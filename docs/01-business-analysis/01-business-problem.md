# Business Problem

## 1. Business Background

Harvest is a supermarket that performs day-to-day retail operations including purchasing goods from suppliers, receiving goods, maintaining inventory, selling goods to customers, processing payments, handling returns, and preparing information required by management.

The supermarket handles a large number of items, suppliers, customers, purchases, stock movements, and sales transactions. These activities are interrelated and require accurate and timely information for efficient operation.

## 2. Current Situation

The supermarket currently depends on manual or partially computerized methods to manage several business activities.

Sales, inventory, supplier information, purchasing, goods receiving, customer information, and payments may be maintained separately. Information generated by one business activity is therefore not always immediately available to another activity.

For example, a sale affects available inventory, receiving goods from a supplier increases inventory, and customer or supplier returns affect both inventory and related transactions. When these activities are handled separately, maintaining consistent and accurate information becomes difficult.

Management also requires timely information about sales, purchases, inventory levels, suppliers, customers, and other operational activities in order to make effective decisions.

## 3. Identified Business Problems

|ID|Business Problem|Description|
|-|-|-|
|BP-01|Inaccurate Inventory Information|Stock quantities may not accurately reflect purchases, sales, returns, goods received, and stock adjustments.|
|BP-02|Inefficient Sales Processing|Manual or disconnected sales activities can increase transaction time and create errors when processing customer purchases.|
|BP-03|Poor Purchasing and Supplier Coordination|Supplier information, purchase orders, and received goods are difficult to track consistently when maintained separately.|
|BP-04|Disconnected Business Information|Sales, inventory, purchasing, customer, supplier, and payment information is not maintained through an integrated process.|
|BP-05|Limited Management Information|Preparing accurate and timely operational information and reports requires additional manual effort.|
|BP-06|Limited Accountability and Traceability|It can be difficult to determine who performed important business transactions or changes.|
|BP-07|Inadequate Access Control|Different employees have different responsibilities, but access to business information and operations may not be sufficiently controlled according to those responsibilities.|

## 4. Business Impact

|Problem ID|Business Impact|
|-|-|
|BP-01|Incorrect stock information can result in stock shortages, unnecessary purchasing, lost sales, and poor inventory decisions.|
|BP-02|Slow or inaccurate sales processing can increase customer waiting time and create transaction errors.|
|BP-03|Poor purchasing control can cause ordering errors, difficulty tracking outstanding orders, and discrepancies between ordered and received goods.|
|BP-04|Disconnected information causes duplicated work, inconsistent records, and difficulty coordinating business activities.|
|BP-05|Management may not receive timely and reliable information required for operational and business decisions.|
|BP-06|Lack of transaction traceability makes errors, unauthorized changes, and operational problems more difficult to investigate.|
|BP-07|Inadequate access control can allow users to perform activities outside their responsibilities and increase business and information-security risks.|

## 5. Problem Statement

Harvest Supermarket requires an integrated and reliable approach to managing its major retail operations. The current dependence on manual or disconnected processes makes it difficult to maintain accurate inventory, efficiently coordinate sales and purchasing activities, obtain timely management information, and ensure appropriate accountability and access control.

The business therefore requires improved integration, accuracy, efficiency, traceability, and control across its core supermarket operations.
