# Business Objectives

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the business improvements expected from the Harvest Supermarket Management System.

The objectives are derived from the business problems identified during business analysis and focus on business outcomes rather than specific implementation technologies.

## 3. Objective Details

### BO-01 — Improve Inventory Accuracy

Maintain accurate and up-to-date inventory information by ensuring that relevant business activities such as goods receiving, sales, returns, and stock adjustments are reflected in inventory records.

**Business Value:**
Improved inventory accuracy reduces stock discrepancies, supports better purchasing decisions, reduces stock shortages, and improves product availability.

### BO-02 — Improve Sales Processing

Improve the efficiency and accuracy of customer sales transactions.

**Business Value:**
Efficient sales processing reduces transaction errors and customer waiting time while improving the overall customer experience.

### BO-03 — Improve Purchasing and Supplier Management

Improve the management of suppliers, purchase orders, goods received, and related purchasing activities.

**Business Value:**
Better purchasing control improves supplier coordination, reduces ordering errors, and allows the supermarket to track the relationship between ordered and received goods.

### BO-04 — Integrate Core Supermarket Operations

Establish an integrated flow of information between inventory, purchasing, goods receiving, sales, returns, customers, suppliers, and payments.

**Business Value:**
Integration reduces duplicated work and inconsistent information while improving coordination between business activities.

### BO-05 — Improve Management Information

Provide timely and reliable information about important supermarket operations.

**Business Value:**
Management can make better operational and business decisions using accurate information about sales, inventory, purchasing, suppliers, customers, and related activities.

### BO-06 — Improve Accountability and Traceability

Improve the ability to identify and trace significant business transactions and activities performed by users.

**Business Value:**
Improved traceability supports auditing, investigation of errors, accountability, and operational control.

### BO-07 — Improve Control over User Access

Ensure that users are able to access business information and perform operations according to their assigned responsibilities.

**Business Value:**
Appropriate access control reduces unauthorized activities and protects important business information and operations.

## 4. Success Indicators

|Objective ID|Success Indicator|
|-|-|
|BO-01|Inventory records are consistently updated through relevant stock-affecting business transactions.|
|BO-02|Sales transactions can be completed accurately through a consistent sales process.|
|BO-03|Suppliers, purchase orders, and received goods can be consistently recorded and tracked.|
|BO-04|Information generated by related business processes is shared without unnecessary duplicate entry.|
|BO-05|Required operational and management information can be obtained from integrated business records.|
|BO-06|Significant transactions can be associated with the users and activities responsible for them.|
|BO-07|Business operations can be restricted according to defined user responsibilities.|
