# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1\. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to identify the individuals and groups that have an interest in, influence on, or are affected by the Harvest Supermarket Management System.

Stakeholders include both people who directly use the system and parties who are affected by its operation without necessarily interacting with it directly.

## 2\. Stakeholder Identification

|ID|Stakeholder|Type|Primary Interest / Relationship|
|-|-|-|-|
|ST-01|Supermarket Owner|Internal|Business performance, profitability, control, and investment value|
|ST-02|Store Manager|Internal|Effective management of supermarket operations|
|ST-03|Cashiers|Internal|Efficient and accurate sales processing|
|ST-04|Inventory Staff|Internal|Accurate item and inventory management|
|ST-05|Purchasing Staff|Internal|Supplier, purchasing, and goods-receiving operations|
|ST-06|Administrative Staff|Internal|User administration and controlled system operation|
|ST-07|Customers|External|Accurate, efficient, and reliable service|
|ST-08|Suppliers|External|Accurate purchase orders, deliveries, and supplier transactions|
|ST-09|Regulatory / Government Authorities|External|Compliance with applicable business, financial, and regulatory requirements|

## 3\. Stakeholder Analysis

### ST-01 — Supermarket Owner

**Responsibilities / Relationship:**  
Owns the business and has overall interest in its performance and sustainability.

**Interests and Expectations:**

* Reliable business information
* Effective operational control
* Reduced losses and errors
* Improved business performance

**Influence:** High  
**Interest:** High  
**Engagement:** Manage Closely

### ST-02 — Store Manager

**Responsibilities / Relationship:**  
Manages day-to-day supermarket operations and supervises operational staff.

**Interests and Expectations:**

* Accurate operational information
* Inventory visibility
* Effective purchasing and sales control
* Appropriate reports
* Employee accountability

**Influence:** High  
**Interest:** High  
**Engagement:** Manage Closely

### ST-03 — Cashiers

**Responsibilities / Relationship:**  
Perform customer sales and related point-of-sale activities.

**Interests and Expectations:**

* Fast transaction processing
* Accurate item and price information
* Simple and reliable sales operations
* Correct payment processing

**Influence:** Medium  
**Interest:** High  
**Engagement:** Keep Informed

### ST-04 — Inventory Staff

**Responsibilities / Relationship:**  
Maintain items and inventory-related information and participate in stock-control activities.

**Interests and Expectations:**

* Accurate stock information
* Efficient item maintenance
* Traceable inventory movements
* Reduced stock discrepancies

**Influence:** Medium  
**Interest:** High  
**Engagement:** Keep Informed

### ST-05 — Purchasing Staff

**Responsibilities / Relationship:**  
Coordinate suppliers, purchasing, purchase orders, and goods-receiving activities.

**Interests and Expectations:**

* Accurate supplier information
* Effective purchase-order processing
* Visibility of goods received
* Reliable purchasing records

**Influence:** Medium  
**Interest:** High  
**Engagement:** Keep Informed

### ST-06 — Administrative Staff

**Responsibilities / Relationship:**  
Support administration of users, roles, privileges, and selected system configuration activities.

**Interests and Expectations:**

* Controlled user management
* Appropriate access assignment
* Reliable administrative operations

**Influence:** Medium  
**Interest:** High  
**Engagement:** Keep Informed

### ST-07 — Customers

**Responsibilities / Relationship:**  
Purchase goods and receive services from the supermarket.

**Interests and Expectations:**

* Fast service
* Accurate prices and bills
* Correct payments and returns
* Product availability

**Influence:** Low  
**Interest:** Medium  
**Engagement:** Monitor

### ST-08 — Suppliers

**Responsibilities / Relationship:**  
Supply goods to the supermarket according to purchasing arrangements.

**Interests and Expectations:**

* Accurate purchase orders
* Accurate delivery records
* Clear supplier transactions
* Effective business relationship

**Influence:** Medium  
**Interest:** Medium  
**Engagement:** Keep Informed

### ST-09 — Regulatory / Government Authorities

**Responsibilities / Relationship:**  
Establish or enforce applicable legal, financial, taxation, and business requirements.

**Interests and Expectations:**

* Regulatory compliance
* Accurate business records where required
* Appropriate transaction records

**Influence:** High  
**Interest:** Low to Medium  
**Engagement:** Keep Satisfied
